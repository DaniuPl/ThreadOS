# The ThreadOS name and mark

The code of ThreadOS is free to use under the MIT licence. The name is
not part of that licence. "ThreadOS" and the ThreadOS mark
([art/threados-mark.svg](art/threados-mark.svg)) are trademarks of the
project's owner (`DaniuPl`), used to tell people what comes from this
project. They are not registered yet; this file says how they may be
used in the meantime and afterwards.

The point is not to stop anyone from using the code. It is that someone
who installs, buys or recommends "ThreadOS" should get what this project
released, and not a modified system or a device that only borrows the
name.

## Allowed without asking

- **Saying what is true.** "Runs on ThreadOS", "a driver for ThreadOS",
  "based on ThreadOS", "a fork of ThreadOS", articles, reviews, videos,
  talks, courses, screenshots and benchmarks.
- **Passing on unmodified releases** (the images and updates published on
  this repository's releases page) under their name, with their
  `LICENSES.txt`.
- **Building from this repository's source unchanged**, for yourself or
  for others, and calling the result ThreadOS.
- **Community material** (user groups, chat servers, wikis, stickers made
  for yourself) that uses the name or the mark and does not claim to be
  official.

## Ask first

- **A modified system or a fork named "ThreadOS"**, or a name that is
  easy to confuse with it ("ThreadOS Pro", "ThreadOS-X", "Thread OS").
  A fork is welcome. Give it a name of its own, and say "based on
  ThreadOS" if you like.
- **Products, devices and services** sold or offered under the name or
  with the mark: hardware that comes with ThreadOS installed, paid
  support, hosting, app stores.
- **Domain names, app names and social accounts** that contain the name.
- **The mark changed** (other colours, cropped, combined with another
  logo), or used in a way that suggests the project endorses you.

Ask in an issue on this repository, or on the project's Discord, before
doing any of these. Permission is given in writing and can be limited
to one use.

## For forks: what to change

Before publishing a modified system under another name, change the
name the system shows (the loading screen, Settings -> About, the
installer, window titles) and replace the mark. The MIT licence still
requires you to keep the copyright notices of the code. That is
attribution, not use of the trademark, and the two do not conflict.
