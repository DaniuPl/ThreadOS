ThreadOS design language

Weave

Cloth, not glass. Surfaces are opaque and carry a faint grain; whatever has focus carries a coloured thread you can follow across the screen.

01

Cloth, not glass

No blur, no transparency behind text. A 3×3 grain tile is copied over a flat fill: on a software compositor that is a memcpy, where blur is a convolution every frame.

02

One thread

Focus is one colour, drawn as a line: the selvedge on top of the window in front, its segment on the loom, the knot by the selected row. Nothing else uses it.

03

Knots for state

A small circle means "this is so": running, unread, the desktop you are on. Hollow is empty, ringed has windows, filled is here.

04

Stitches divide

A dashed line separates parts of one surface; a solid line is the edge of a surface. Fields have a stitch under them that turns into the thread when they take focus.
