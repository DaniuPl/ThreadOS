ProtonUI v2: the toolkit
every ThreadOS window is made of

v1 is what programs use today: user/udraw.h (shapes and text, drawn by the desktop) and user/ugui.h (controls as a list of things). v2 keeps both - every v1 program builds unchanged - and adds what Weave needs: the dyes and the grain as theme values, knots, stitches and threads as drawing operations, the controls the mock-ups use, touch, and a way into the shuttle.

Touch

pu_density() says how the window is used: pointer (rows 28 px, targets 24 px) or finger (rows 48 px, targets 44 px). Layout asks it, so one program serves the desktop, the tablet and the phone. A long press is a right click; a swipe on a list row brings its actions out.

Keyboard and reading

Tab goes in the order things were added; the focus is the thread, 2 px outside the control. Every item has a name (a button's caption, a field's label, pu_name() for an icon alone), which a screen reader can ask for. Text keeps 4.5 to 1 against its surface in both themes.

Tests

A new group, t_protonui, boots a demo window with every control and reads the screen through QMP: the selvedge's colour along the top, the grain's 3x3 pattern, each knot's three states, the thread under the focused control. Sizes come from plain #defines that scripts/ptest.py reads, as it does for v1. The groups that click v1 programs (t_gcode and the rest) must pass unchanged.
